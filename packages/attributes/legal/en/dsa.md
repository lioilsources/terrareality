## 1. Single point of contact

Under Regulation (EU) 2022/2065 (Digital Services Act, DSA) the operator of TerraReality, [company name], ID No. [fill in], designates a single point of contact:

- **for Member State authorities, the Commission and the European Board for Digital Services (Art. 11 DSA):** [dsa@terrareality.cz]
- **for users (Art. 12 DSA):** [dsa@terrareality.cz], or the support form in the app. A human answers, not a bot.

You can write in **Czech or English**. The Digital Services Coordinator in the Czech Republic is the Czech Telecommunication Office (ČTÚ).

## 2. How to report illegal content (Art. 16 DSA)

Report an illegal listing, photo, profile or message by e-mail to [dsa@terrareality.cz] (subject "Notice of illegal content") and include:

1. the link (URL) or identifier of the listing or message,
2. why you consider the content illegal,
3. your name and e-mail (not required for offences against children),
4. a statement that the notice is made in good faith and that the information is accurate and complete.

You can report an inappropriate chat message directly with the "Report" button in the conversation. [A "Report listing" button on the listing page is being prepared.]

We confirm receipt by e-mail. Notices are assessed by a human moderator; automated tools and AI may only flag content for review and **never decide** on removal themselves. We inform you of the decision without undue delay.

## 3. Moderation and statement of reasons (Art. 17 DSA)

Listings are approved by moderation before publication. If we reject, hide or remove a listing or a message, or restrict an account, we tell the affected user the reason, the legal or contractual ground, whether an automated tool was used and the available redress.

## 4. Complaints and out-of-court settlement (Art. 20–21 DSA)

You can object to a moderation decision within 6 months at [dsa@terrareality.cz]; a different person than the original moderator reviews it. You may also turn to a certified out-of-court dispute settlement body or to a court.
