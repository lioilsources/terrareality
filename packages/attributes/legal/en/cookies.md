## 1. What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores in your browser. The mobile apps do not use cookies; they keep similar data (sign-in) in the device's secure storage.

## 2. Cookies we use

We use **only strictly necessary cookies** without which the website does not work (sign-in, security, language, remembering that you closed this notice). They do not require your consent (Section 89(3) of Act No. 127/2005 Coll., Art. 5(3) ePrivacy Directive). **We use no analytics, advertising or tracking cookies** and embed no third-party content that sets cookies.

## 3. Optional cookies in the future

If we ever introduce analytics or other optional cookies, they will be **off by default** and used only with your prior consent, which you can withdraw on this page at any time.

## 4. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings. If you block strictly necessary cookies, you cannot sign in.
